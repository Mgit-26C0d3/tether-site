TETHER

Hold to grab. Let go to fly.

A one-thumb swinging game for iPhone. Five worlds are coming apart behind you, and the only way out is forward.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone · Pay once · No ads

How it plays

One thumb. That is the whole game.

There is no stick and no second button to learn. Everything you do comes down to when you hold and when you let go.

  1. 01

    Hold

    Your tether fires at the next bar and pulls you into a swing.

  2. 02

    Let go

    Release at the right moment and the swing throws you forward. Release perfectly and it throws you further.

  3. 03

    Keep moving

    Speed builds your multiplier, all the way to ×10. Lose your rhythm and it breaks. Slow down and the collapse catches you.

Five worlds

Every world changes the rules.

Between swings you hit the ground running: jump gaps, stomp energy cores, ride zip lines and dodge falling rock. Then each world adds a move of its own.

And a winter world that opens every December.

Four ways to play

Five minutes or fifty.

Daily Run

Take the top score on today's level.

One level a day, the same for every player in the world. A new one at midnight UTC.

Minute Dash

Sixty seconds. Best score wins.

Short enough for a queue, and it has its own daily leaderboard.

Endless

Go until the collapse gets you.

All five worlds flowing one into the next, or a single world for as long as you can last.

Free Run

Your rules.

Tune the physics, replay any level by its code, or practise one move at a time in the training room.
