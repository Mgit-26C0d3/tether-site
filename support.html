Playing

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to fire your tether at the next bar. You swing while you hold. Let go to fly, then hold again to catch the next bar.

On the ground, in a run section, a tap is a hop and a longer hold is a higher jump. The game's Settings has a How To tab with every move, and Free Run has a training room where nothing ends the run.

What is chasing me?

The collapse: a beam that follows you from the left and speeds up as the run goes on. If it reaches you, the run is over. There is also a void below, so falling ends the run too.

How does the score work?

Distance and time earn points, and both are multiplied by your multiplier, which runs from ×1 to ×10 and builds with speed. Clean and perfect releases, chained grabs, loops and other skilful moves push it up faster. Losing momentum breaks it.

How do power-ups work?

You collect them in run sections. Press the bolt button to use one: time slows while you choose. The How To tab in Settings lists what each one does.

Why can't I pause the Daily Run or Minute Dash?

Those two are ranked, and every player gets the same level, so pausing is off to keep it fair. If a call or a notification interrupts a run, the game holds it and counts you back in when you return. Endless runs in a single world and Free Run can be paused.

Scores and leaderboards

When does the Daily Run change?

At midnight UTC, for everyone at once. The tile on the main menu counts down to the next one. Minute Dash changes at the same time.

My score is not on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Check that you are signed in: open the iPhone's Settings, then Game Center. Scores are sent when the phone is online.

A Daily Run only counts for the day it was started on. A run that begins before midnight UTC and ends after it is not posted.

Can I play without Game Center?

Yes. Everything works without it. You keep your own best scores on the phone, and only the shared leaderboards are missing.

Progress and unlocks

How do I open more worlds?

With mastery stars. Each of the five worlds has five goals, and each goal you finish earns a star. Ember Foundry opens at 2 stars, Glass Expanse at 5, Skybound Isles at 9 and Astral Divide at 14. The Daily Run, Minute Dash and the Endless tour pass through every world from the start, so you can earn stars for worlds you have not opened yet.

How do I earn skins?

Each world has a skin in three pieces. The trail comes with that world's first star, the core with its third, and the ring with its fifth. The Progress screen, behind the star button on the main menu, shows what is next.

When can I play the winter world?

Solstice Hollow is open from 1 December to 6 January, by your phone's own clock. Its tile appears on the main menu during those weeks.

Does my progress move to a new phone?

Yes, if you are signed in to iCloud on both. Stars, unlocks and bests are kept in your own iCloud and joined together across your devices. If progress has not appeared, check that iCloud is switched on for the phone and give it a minute on a good connection.

Sound and comfort

There is no sound.

The game follows the ring and silent switch, so it is quiet when the phone is on silent. Check the volume buttons too, and the four sliders under Settings, then Sound: Master, Music, Effects and Ambience.

Can I listen to my own music while I play?

Yes. Your music keeps playing, and the game turns its own music off while it does. You still hear the game's effects.

The game is using a lot of battery, or the phone gets warm.

Open Settings, then Graphics. Turning off Smooth motion holds the game at 60 frames a second, and the SAVER preset switches off the heaviest effects. Low Power Mode does the first of these for you.

Are there options for comfort and accessibility?

Under Settings, then Play, you can reduce flashing, switch to left-handed controls, and turn off screen shake and vibration. The game also follows the iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.

Buying and requirements

What do I get for the price?

The whole game. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and nothing else to buy later.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

I deleted the game. Do I have to buy it again?

No. Download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account and it is free.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with iOS 18 or later. It plays in portrait and is in English.
