Policy

Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version. TETHER does not collect personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything about you to me or to anyone I work with. What the game saves stays on your iPhone, in your own iCloud, and in Apple's Game Center if you use it.

Who this is from

TETHER: Outrun the Collapse is made by Michael Lameri, an independent developer. "I" and "me" in this policy mean him. You can reach me at madebymike1@icloud.com.

What the game saves on your iPhone

To work as a game, TETHER keeps these on your device:

  • your best scores, statistics and mastery progress;
  • what you have unlocked and the look you have chosen for your character;
  • your settings, such as volume, graphics and controls;
  • a recording of your best run in each mode, used to show its ghost.

None of this identifies you, and none of it is sent to me. Deleting the game removes it from the phone.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game stores your progress in your own private iCloud storage so it can follow you to your other devices. Apple runs that storage. I cannot see it or read it.

To stop it, open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and switch TETHER off in the list of apps that use iCloud.

Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your scores to it, and they appear on the leaderboards under your Game Center name. I can see the same leaderboards any player can see: a name and a score. I receive nothing else about you.

Game Center is Apple's service and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can play without it. To stop using it, sign out in the iPhone's Settings under Game Center.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking software from me or from anyone else.
  • It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • It has no accounts, no chat and no way to share content with other players.

Buying the game

You buy TETHER from Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. I never see your payment details, and the game has no in-app purchases.

Information Apple may share with me

If you have agreed on your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give me crash reports and counts of how the game is used, such as how many people downloaded it. Apple removes anything that identifies you before I see it. You can turn this off in the iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

If you email me

When you write to me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell me. I use it only to reply and to fix the problem you describe. I do not add you to a mailing list or pass your details to anyone. Ask and I will delete our correspondence.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. Its fonts and pictures are served from the site itself, so your browser contacts no one else to show it. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep ordinary server logs, such as the address a request came from, for a short time for security.

Children

TETHER collects no personal data from anyone, which includes children. It has no ads, no purchases inside the game and no contact between players.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: madebymike1@icloud.com.
